Connected Platform · Design direction · Draft 1

Light. Layered. Always in motion.

Keep the colors, type and spacing rhythm from the current system. Replace everything that reads as an admin portal: flat white panels, hard borders, modal wizards and pages that snap from one to the next.

01
Glass, not boxes
Surfaces are frosted layers over a soft, living ground. Depth tells you what's in front. Borders turn into highlights.
02
Out of focus, not gone
Filters, history and context slide behind the main surface like a card deck. One tap brings them back. Nothing is hidden in a menu.
03
Motion explains
Every transition shows where something came from and where it went. A step becomes a grid tile. A dismissed play folds away. A chat edit morphs the asset in place.
04
Lime means ready
Navy is the brand and the human. The logo's lime marks what the AI built and anything ready to launch. It's a signal color, never decoration.

Signature patterns · see the next two boards
Card-deck filters
Filters fan out as a deck beside the content, then tuck behind it with an edge still showing.
Coverflow steps
Guided flows put the current step center stage, with neighbors angled in and still clickable.
Fly to grid
"Review all" sends the same cards into a grid. They're the same objects, just a different view.
Scoped chat sheet
Chat slides in attached to the thing you're editing. It's a revision layer, not a separate help bot.
Fold away
Dismissed items fold and collapse so the list reflows. No toast, no confirm dialog. Undo stays one tap away.
