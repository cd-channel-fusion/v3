Connected Platform 2028

Interactive concept prototypes for an AI-led channel marketing platform: suggested playbooks for each location, a clear path from spending to co-op money received, visible oversight of everything done in a partner's name, and a brand view of where national initiatives actually ran.

01Design language Principles, color, glass tiers, type, motion tokens and the signature interaction patterns. Static 02Location briefing A dealer's home: suggested playbooks with the reasons behind them, filters that tuck behind the main panel, running work and money on the table. InteractiveJ04 · J17 03Running a playbook Steps in a coverflow with include, modify and skip, chat edits, brand checks, review-all grid, and audiences you can open, edit and swap. InteractiveJ04 · J05 · J07.A 04After launch From committing money to co-op cash confirmed received, including a rejected proof the dealer challenges. InteractiveJ07.A → J07 · J15 · T06 05Done on your behalf Everything done in the dealer's name by the platform, their rep, their agency or the brand, with the permission each act relied on and limits they can change. InteractivePT01 · G03 06Brand coverage Corporate's view of a national event: which locations actually ran it, which ran their own version or opted out, and why the rest didn't.
